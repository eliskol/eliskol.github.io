## Course description

A student-led colloquium connecting category theory with logic and the internal languages of structured categories. Topics include categorical semantics of Martin-Löf dependent type theory and their use in proving syntactic results. Led by Tom Lindquist under Stephen Bigelow’s supervision.

See [CCS Fall 2025 course description](https://ccs.ucsb.edu/courses/2025/fall/categories-types-logics) for official course description.
